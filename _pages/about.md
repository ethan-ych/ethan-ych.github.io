Welcome to my personal page! I'm currently a Master's student in Computer Science at Carnegie Mellon University. I recently completed my Bachelor's degree in the same field there, along with a minor in Mathematics.

My academic interests lie in theoretical computer science (Algorithms/Complexity), while I also enjoy studying programming language theory, logic, and math on the side. This website is primarily a place for me to reflect on and write about courses I've taken in these areas, though I may branch out into other topics in the future. 

Outside of academics and work, I enjoy bouldering and steeping/tasting various kinds of tea (my favorite tea is lightly roasted Taiwanese high mountain oolong).
